Build 1.0.0 (3) - Leruma flavor - 31 Jul 2026

Smoke test checklist:
1. Log in and confirm the app connects to https://leruma.co.tz/api
   (NOT saichi.co.tz - that is the fallback if the FLAVOR define failed)
2. Confirm the commission dashboard loads on the home screen
3. Confirm the drawer shows "Financial Banking" and "Trade", and does NOT
   show "Cash Submit" or plain "Banking"
4. Confirm sales screen shows the Sheet button and inline cart preview
5. Confirm NFC card lookup works
6. General pass on Android 14+ (targetSdk raised 35 -> 36 in this build)

Build config:
  flutter build appbundle --flavor leruma --dart-define=FLAVOR=leruma --release
  package      co.tz.leruma.pos
  versionCode  20
  versionName  1.0.0
  minSdk       24 (Android 7.0)
  targetSdk    36 (Android 16)
